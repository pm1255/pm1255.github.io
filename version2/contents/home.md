Miao Pan is a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/) (2023–2028), supervised by [Prof. Xuhong Zhang](https://person.zju.edu.cn/zhangxuhong) and co-advised by [Prof. Wenqi Zhang](https://person.zju.edu.cn/wenqizhang). Previously, he was also advised by [Prof. Jiayu Pan](https://jiayupan26.github.io/). He received his B.E. degree in Data Science and Big Data Technology from Zhejiang Gongshang University (2019–2023).

My research explores reliable multimodal and embodied intelligence through three connected directions:

1. **Agentic systems:** organizing task-relevant context and connecting grounded reasoning, planning, and execution so high-level goals become useful actions.
2. **Multimodal foundation models:** building models and training recipes for visual understanding, spatial reasoning, and action learning across tasks and environments.
3. **Trustworthy AI and networks:** designing evaluations and secure systems that make intelligent agents more reliable in real-world settings.

My goal is to build intelligent agents that understand complex environments and act reliably to assist people in everyday scenarios.
